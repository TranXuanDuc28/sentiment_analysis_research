Multilingual Word Embeddings 
multilingual word representations in vector spaceallowing words from different languages to be mapped into the same vector spaceThis means that words with equivalent or nearly identical meanings in different languages will be represented by vectors close to each other in this space.
--- Page 9 ---
Multilingual Word Embeddings: properties
General semantic word mapWords with similar meanings will be represented close to each other, regardless of languageEX: "apple" in English and "manzana" in Spanish can be represented by vectors that are close to each other.
Applied in multilingual problemsmachine translationlanguage classificationmultilingual searchnatural language understanding systems that support multiple languages

Multilingual embeddings: Training methods•Translate words directly•Use a bilingual dictionary or bilingual data to map words between languages•Training with concurrent models•Train a single model with data from multiple languages to create a common vector space•Fine-tuning on additional languages•Extends a monolingual model by fine-tuning with multilingual data to preserve semantics in the vector space.

Cross-lingual Transfer Learning
•Transfer knowledge from one language to another•NLP models can learn from a source language (ex: English), and then apply that knowledge to a target language (Vietnamese), without having to be completely retrained. 
--- Page 12 ---
Cross-lingual Transfer Learning: Methods
Based on multilingual models mBERT, XLM-R multilingual models trained on data from multiple languageslearning common linguistic representations, allowing knowledge transfer between languages.
Fine-tuning for target languageuses a training model from the source language, then fine-tunes it with a small amount of data from the target language to improve transfer.
